Help & FAQs

Short, silent demos of how to get around the Observer. Pick a topic, watch it once, then try it yourself. Questions we have not covered? Use Contact Us at the bottom of any page.

On your phoneOn a tablet, laptop or desktop

Open the menu

  1. Tap the three lines at the top right of any page.
  2. Search the Observer, jump Home, log in, or pick any page from the lists.
  3. Scroll down inside the menu for contact details, reviews and social links.
  4. Tap Close to return.
On your phoneOn a tablet, laptop or desktop

Choose a reading voice

  1. In the menu, tap the round speaker button next to Light / Dark and Aa.
  2. A list of English voices appears. Tap any voice to hear a sample of it.
  3. Above the list, Deeper and Higher change the pitch, and Slower and Faster change the pace. Mix and match until the Observer sounds like your kind of reader.
  4. The voice you tap stays selected; tap Close to return to the menu. Your choice is remembered on this phone or computer for next time.
  5. The voices come from your own device, so each phone or computer shows its own list. Device default is whatever voice it normally uses.
  6. Be bold, and experiment. Nothing here can break the Observer, every setting is one tap from undone, and the right voice and pace can turn a long read into a pleasure. Think of it as adjusting your reading chair: nobody gets it perfect on the first try, and nobody gets hurt trying.
On your phoneOn a tablet, laptop or desktop

Find any story

  1. Open the menu and type a word or name in the Search the Observer box.
  2. Tap Search. The results list every matching story, with your words highlighted.
  3. Tap a result and the words you searched for stay highlighted inside the article, so you can spot them quickly.
On your phoneOn a tablet, laptop or desktop

Light or dark, and bigger text

  1. Open the menu on any article.
  2. Light / Dark switches the whole site between a bright and a dark look.
  3. The Aa button makes article text larger; press again to step up, and it cycles back to normal.
  4. At the larger text sizes, the menu and the footer at the bottom of the page switch from two columns of link buttons to a single column, so every button stays easy to read and tap.
  5. Treat these as dials, not decisions. Spin them, squint at them, change your mind. A little tinkering makes reading easier on your eyes and richer in every way, and nothing you try can hurt the page.
  6. Close the menu and keep reading.
On your phoneOn a tablet, laptop or desktop

Swipe through each section

  1. On a phone, a tablet, or a touchscreen laptop or desktop, swipe a section left or right with your finger to see more stories.
  2. Or press the arrows at the sides of the section. With a mouse, click the arrows.
  3. Latest News, National Columnists, the Magazine, Observercast and Newsmakers all work this way.
On your phoneOn a tablet, laptop or desktop

Open a whole section

  1. Tap or click a section name on the home page, like National Columnists or The Magazine.
  2. You land on that section's archive with every story filed there.
  3. Use the browser back button to return home.
On your phoneOn a tablet, laptop or desktop

Meet the writers

  1. On the home page, tap or click a columnist's picture under Featured Columnists.
  2. You land on their author page with a short bio and every article they have written.
On your phoneOn a tablet, laptop or desktop

Move between stories

  1. On a phone, a tablet, or a touchscreen laptop or desktop, swipe left or right across the post title or the bar under it to jump to the newer or older story.
  2. Or tap or click the triangles at the left and right of the bar under the title.
  3. The center button shares the article.
On your phoneOn a tablet, laptop or desktop

Have a story read to you

  1. Open any article. The round speaker button sits at the bottom right corner of the screen.
  2. Tap or click it once and the Observer reads the story aloud. The button turns gold and pulses while it is reading, and the sentence being read is softly highlighted so you can follow along.
  3. To stop, tap the button again, tap or click anywhere else on the page, or press the Esc key on a keyboard.
  4. It also stops by itself at the end of the story, and when you leave the page.
On your phoneOn a tablet, laptop or desktop

Share any article

  1. Open an article and tap or click the share icon in the bar under the title.
  2. Your phone, tablet, laptop or desktop shows its own share panel: send to a device, show a QR code, or pick an app.
  3. The link that arrives shows the article picture, headline and a short description.

As it appears in a text message or chat. Each app draws it a little differently, but it always carries the article picture, headline and a short description.
